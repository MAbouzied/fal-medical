# Surface brief: Blog (Read mode)

## Mode

Read — comprehension, trust, comfortable reading, and wayfinding over decorative complexity.

## Audience

Arabic-speaking visitors researching dental, dermatology, laser, or preventive-care topics before contacting Fal Clinic in Riyadh.

## Purpose

Help visitors understand common clinic topics in clear Arabic, build trust in Fal Clinic’s expertise, and offer a natural path to book after reading.

## Reading flow

1. Listing: breadcrumb + short intro → one featured article → recent grid → booking CTA
2. Article: breadcrumb → large H1 → meta (date · reading time · author) → dominant cover → sparse body → related posts → booking CTA

## Delivery

- Arabic only (`locale: "ar"`)
- Routes: `/blogs`, `/blogs/[slug]`, optional `/blogs/page/[page]`
- No `/en/blogs` counterpart
- Language switch from blog pages returns to `/en`

## Conversion

Booking CTA (`BookingSection`) is the final conversion point on listing and article pages. No competing promo clusters in the reading column.

## Visual authority

Extension of Fal Clinic — Tajawal, teal primary, mint surfaces, navy ink, existing radii/shadows/spacing — not a separate magazine identity.
