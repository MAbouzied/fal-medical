# Surface brief: Blog article

## Mode

Read

## Scope

`/blogs/[slug]` detail page only. Listing stays as-is.

## Direction (user-pinned)

Title-led editorial shell matching the staging reference hierarchy:

1. Breadcrumb
2. Large H1 title
3. Meta row: date · reading time · author (with avatar when available)
4. Dominant cover image
5. Sparse body (short takeaway, not a long essay shell)
6. Related posts → booking CTA

## Constraints

- Fal Clinic visual identity (Tajawal, teal, mint, navy) — extension, not a magazine rebrand
- Arabic-only
- One H1
- Comfortable reading measure; generous vertical space after removing dense article filler
