# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are Arabic-speaking residents of Riyadh (Al Yarmouk and nearby districts) looking for dental, dermatology, aesthetic, nutrition, obstetrics & gynecology, or physiotherapy care.

Secondary users include English-speaking visitors who need clinic basics (services, doctors, contact, booking). Blog stays Arabic-only at launch.

## Product Purpose

Fal Clinic (مجمع عيادات فال الطبية) is a bilingual clinic site that explains services, introduces doctors, and converts visitors into booked appointments via WhatsApp and the booking form.

## Positioning

A Riyadh medical complex on Imam Abdullah bin Saud Street, Al Yarmouk — not a copy of Beauty Corner’s look, and not a multi-city marketplace.

## Operating Context

- Public marketing site built with Astro, deployed on Cloudflare
- Arabic is the default locale; English covers core clinic routes
- Blog launches Arabic-only at `/blogs`
- Booking and contact are the main conversion paths
- Visual system: white and mint surfaces, teal (`#12a394`), navy ink (`#10182d`), 8px buttons

## Brand Commitments

- Brand names: مجمع عيادات فال الطبية / Fal Clinic
- Do not reuse Beauty Corner colors, logo, doctor identities, or license numbers
- Medical claims must stay conservative and marked for clinical review

## Constraints

- Do not invent testimonials, unverified outcomes, or fake named doctors
- English blog routes are out of scope for launch
- SEO must not emit English alternates for Arabic-only blog URLs
- `SEO_INDEXABLE` stays false until licenses, maps, doctors, and content are confirmed
